## Project Overview

Sonámbulo Estudio Creativo defines itself as a **"super group of creatives, advertisers, marketers and idea nerds"** that specializes in securing brands from construction to final consumer. The website reflects this philosophy through an innovative and immersive digital experience.

## Key Features

### 🎨 Design and Visual Experience
- **Cohesive visual identity** with characteristic colors (lime, blue, ink)
- **Custom typography** Montserrat and Darker Grotesque
- **Iconography system** with asterisks as distinctive elements
- **Fluid navigation** with smooth scrolling and seamless transitions

### ⚡ Advanced Animations
- **Scroll-triggered animations** with Framer Motion
- **Parallax effects** and 3D transformations
- **Text animations** with character-by-character reveal
- **Interactive particles** and custom cursor effects

### 📱 Responsive & Interactive
- **Fully responsive design** for all devices
- **Smart touch vs hover detection** to optimize interactions
- **Animated mobile menu** with morphing transitions
- **Optimized touch gestures** for mobile

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- **Next.js 14** with App Router and React Server Components
- **TypeScript** for type safety
- **Framer Motion** for performant animations
- **Custom hooks** for scroll tracking and device detection

### Key Components
- **Sticky scroll sections** with progress tracking
- **Dynamic portfolio system** for case studies
- **Interactive capability cards** with expand/collapse
- **Multi-layer animations** for depth effects

### Optimizations
- **Image optimization** with Next.js Image and lazy loading
- **Performance monitoring** with Core Web Vitals
- **SEO optimization** with dynamic metadata
- **Progressive enhancement** for better accessibility

## Main Sections

### 1. Hero & About
Implementation of an innovative sticky scroll system where content transforms as the user navigates, revealing the studio's value proposition step by step.

### 2. Process and Methodology
Interactive visualization of the studio's workflow, from brand construction to final consumer, with expandable cards and hover animations.

### 3. Capabilities
Three main areas of specialization:
- **SNMBL Channel Development** - Business Strategy and Trade Marketing
- **SNMBL Data & Intelligence Center** - Dashboards and web/mobile development
- **SNMBL Ideas Lab** - Creative Strategy and Brand Building

### 4. Dynamic Portfolio
Case study system with:
- **Custom hero sections** per client
- **Responsive image carousel**
- **Insight cards** with different layouts
- **Fluid navigation** between projects

## Technical Achievements

- ✅ **Immersive experience** with fluid 60fps animations
- ✅ **Optimized performance** with high Lighthouse scores
- ✅ **Enhanced accessibility** with keyboard navigation and screen readers
- ✅ **SEO optimized** with dynamic metadata and structured data
- ✅ **Mobile-first** with native touch interactions

## Impact

The Sonámbulo Estudio Creativo website sets a new standard for corporate sites in the creative industry, combining visual storytelling, advanced technology, and exceptional user experience to effectively communicate the studio's unique value proposition.
