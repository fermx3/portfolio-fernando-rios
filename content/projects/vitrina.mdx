## Overview

Built first for piercing and tattoo studios, which sell jewelry and supplies to the public and to other studios and use some of them in their own work. Vertical presets adapt it to other retailers.

## Technical Implementation

### Data

Stock is an append-only ledger with materialized balances, and money is stored in cents. Constraints, triggers and RPCs keep the rules in the database, where pgTAP tests them.

### Tenancy

Each business runs on its own subdomain with its own logo, accent color and title font. RLS separates tenants, and roles set what owners, admins, staff and viewers can do.

### Delivery

Decisions are recorded as ADRs. CI runs unit, integration and end-to-end tests and deploys production in order.

## Features

- Catalog with variants, vertical presets and CSV import
- Stock by location, lot and serial number, with expiry alerts
- Counter with public and studio price lists
- Wholesale orders and customer price lists
- Dashboard with low stock and suggested reorders grouped by supplier
- Branding settings with a live preview and a contrast check
