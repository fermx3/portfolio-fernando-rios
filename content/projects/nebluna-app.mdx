## Project Overview

**Nebluna** is the operations system and online store of a specialty coffee micro-roastery in Mexico City. It covers the full product cycle: green coffee intake, roasting, bulk stock, packaging, sales across configurable channels, the online store, shipping and reporting. It is a monorepo with two **Next.js 16** (App Router) apps in **TypeScript** on a single **Supabase** database, and it replaces the spreadsheet the business had been running on.

## Technical Implementation

### Data Model & Integrity

230 versioned migrations define the schema in **PostgreSQL**. Business rules are enforced in the database rather than in the forms: constraints and triggers prevent stock from going negative, every sale references a specific packaged-inventory row, and a blend deducts from its source lots through a single canonical path. Supplies are costed FIFO by layers, so registering a purchase does not rewrite the margin of months already reported. Business dates are stored as `DATE` rather than `TIMESTAMPTZ`, and rows are always related by foreign key, never by matching text.

### Roles & Security

Two roles with different products: administration sees financials, costs and settings; each sales rep sees the catalogue, their own sales and their own commissions, and nothing belonging to anyone else. The separation is enforced by **Row Level Security** policies on every table, so it holds even if a route guard fails. New users join by invitation, and removing one closes their access.

### Online Store

A second app in `apps/tienda`, on the same database and deployed separately. The customer browses the catalogue, picks ground or whole bean, pays by bank transfer or through **Mercado Pago Checkout Pro**, and follows the order from a link. The account is the verified email: the customer signs in with a six-digit code, with no registration, on tables separate from the panel's Auth. Orders and codes are rate limited per IP, stored as an HMAC rather than in plain text. Coupons, a free-shipping threshold and reviews with a public link are managed from the panel, and the owner is notified by email through **Resend** of orders, payments and reviews.

### Roast Log

Each roast can record its target before roasting, the green coffee analysis, the curve, and a sensory evaluation afterwards. The curve is captured by hand on the phone in real time, with a clock that survives a reload and the rate of rise drawn live, or imported from an **Artisan** `.alog` file. A roast can follow a reference curve, and when registering the next roast of the same coffee the app shows what was decided last time. Cupping sessions evaluate several coffees at once, system lots or guest coffees, with external tasters invited by link and blind tasting chosen per session.

### Interface

**React Query** for server state, **react-hook-form** with **Zod** for forms, and **shadcn/ui** over **Tailwind CSS 4**. Charts in **Recharts**. A print group renders a letter-sized sale ticket and a 15 × 10 cm shipping label, also as a PDF. Mobile first, with the dense screens, reports and reconciliation, designed for a desktop.

### Testing & CI

883 unit tests, 191 component tests and 85 integration suites covering RLS policies, the sale lifecycle, commissions, repackaging, reconciliation, the store and its Mercado Pago payments, and date handling. Five GitHub Actions workflows: lint blocking at zero errors, integration against a Supabase stack built from scratch inside the runner, a weekly Lighthouse audit, a guard that compares the migrations against the remote registry, and the nightly backup. Integration only runs when a pull request touches the database or its tests.

## Functionality

- **Sales**: a guided flow, configurable channels with the percentage and fixed fee each one charges (Mercado Libre included), retail and wholesale pricing, partial payments, customer credit, cancellation and a printable ticket.
- **Online store**: catalogue and product page, bag, checkout, Mercado Pago, customer account with past orders and addresses, coupons and reviews.
- **Inventory**: packaged and bulk stock broken down by lot, with resting time and a configurable freshness limit, and bag assignment to sales reps, who confirm receipt in the app.
- **Production**: roast records with green weight, roasted weight, shrinkage and a derived cost per kilo, blends built from several source lots, and the roast log.
- **Cupping**: sessions with several coffees, guest coffees, external tasters and comparison against previous evaluations.
- **Shipping**: delivery decided by the channel, shipment record with tracking and its own label.
- **Finance**: commissions with a settlement history, business expenses split across roasts, and the result for the period.
- **Reconciliation**: physical inventory counts and count requests sent to sales reps.
- **Reports**: by channel, product, customer, freshness, profitability, collections, pricing and sales rep.
