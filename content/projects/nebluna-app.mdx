## Project Overview

**Nebluna** is the operations system and online store of a specialty coffee micro-roastery in Mexico City. It covers green coffee intake, roasting, packaging, sales, shipping and reporting, and replaces the spreadsheet the business ran on. It is a monorepo with two **Next.js** apps in **TypeScript** on a single **Supabase** database.

## Technical Implementation

### Data Model

The schema lives in versioned **PostgreSQL** migrations. Business rules are enforced in the database: stock cannot go negative, every sale points to a specific lot, and a blend deducts from its source lots through a single path. Supplies are costed FIFO, so a new purchase does not rewrite past margins.

### Roles

Administration sees finances, costs and settings. Each sales rep sees the catalogue, their own sales and their own commissions. **Row Level Security** enforces the split on every table.

### Online Store

A second app on the same database. Customers browse the catalogue, choose ground or whole bean, and pay by bank transfer or **Mercado Pago**. Each order becomes a sale in the panel, so there is only one inventory. Customers sign in with a code sent to their email, with no password. Coupons and reviews are managed from the panel.

### Roast Log & Cupping

Each roast can record a target, a curve and a sensory evaluation. The curve is captured on the phone during the roast or imported from **Artisan**, and can be compared against a reference profile. Cupping sessions evaluate several coffees side by side, blind if needed, including coffees from other roasters.

### Testing & CI

Unit, component and integration tests with **Vitest**. GitHub Actions runs lint, integration tests against a database built from scratch, a migration check against production, and a nightly encrypted backup.

## Functionality

- **Sales**: guided flow, configurable channels, retail and wholesale pricing, partial payments and a printable ticket.
- **Online store**: catalogue, checkout, Mercado Pago, customer account, coupons and reviews.
- **Inventory**: packaged and bulk stock by lot, with freshness tracking.
- **Production**: roasts, shrinkage, cost per kilo, blends and the roast log.
- **Cupping**: multi-coffee sessions with comparison between tasters.
- **Shipping**: shipment records with tracking and a printable label.
- **Finance**: commissions, expenses and the result for each period.
- **Reports**: by channel, product, customer, freshness and profitability.
