## Overview

Nebluna replaces the spreadsheet the roastery ran on. Administration and sales reps work in the panel, customers buy in the store, and both apps share the same lots.

## Technical Implementation

### Data Model

Every sale points to a lot, and a blend deducts from its source lots through a single path. Supplies are costed FIFO, so new purchases do not rewrite past margins.

### Online Store

Each order becomes a panel sale, and each payment is recorded once even if it arrives late or twice. Customers pay by bank transfer or Mercado Pago and sign in with an emailed code.

### Roast Log

Each roast records a target, a curve and a sensory evaluation. The curve is captured on a phone or imported from Artisan, then compared against a reference profile.

## Features

- **Sales**: retail and wholesale pricing, partial payments, printable ticket.
- **Store**: checkout, customer account, coupons, reviews.
- **Inventory**: packaged and bulk stock by lot, with freshness.
- **Production**: roasts, shrinkage, blends.
- **Cupping**: blind multi-coffee sessions across tasters.
- **Shipping**: tracking and printable label.
- **Finance**: commissions, expenses, period results.
- **Reports**: by channel, product, customer and profitability.
