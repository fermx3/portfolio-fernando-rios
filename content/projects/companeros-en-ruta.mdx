## Overview

Six roles share the platform: admin, brand manager, supervisor, promoter, sales advisor and client. Each has its own web dashboard; field staff and clients also get a native app.

## Technical Implementation

### Multi-Tenancy

Row Level Security on every table, plus a tenant filter in every query as defense in depth. URLs, exports and QR codes use public identifiers, not internal IDs. A separate suite tests those rules.

### Mobile Apps

The staff app scans QR codes and attaches GPS-tagged photo evidence to visits. Both apps receive push notifications and ship to iOS and Android through EAS Build.

### Loyalty

A sales advisor scans the client's QR to redeem a promotion, and the redemption is logged for distributor-to-brand billing. Points, tiers and brand memberships are tracked per client.

## Features

- **Visits**: three-stage assessment wizard (pricing, inventory, POP) with auto-save.
- **Promotions and surveys**: authored by brands, published after admin approval.
- **Targeting**: audience filters with reach estimation.
- **Operations**: orders, support tickets, exhibition submissions.
- **Notifications**: real-time in-app alerts.
- **Exports**: segmented datasets as ZIP archives.
