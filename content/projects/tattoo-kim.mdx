## Overview

Tattoo Kim is a tattoo and piercing studio with locations in Playa del Carmen and Cancún. The site presents artist portfolios, services, locations and an aftercare blog in Spanish and English, built with Next.js (App Router) and Tailwind CSS.

## Technical Implementation

### Internationalization
`next-intl` handles the `/es` and `/en` prefixes, detects the preferred language and maps localized path segments (`/artistas` and `/artists`).

### Content
Artist profiles and blog posts are MDX files per locale, with frontmatter validated before each build.

### WhatsApp contact
Links are built per location and pre-fill the message with the service or artist being viewed, instead of a contact form.

### Images
`next/image` serves AVIF and WebP with lazy loading and pre-generated blur placeholders.

## Features

- Spanish and English with localized URLs
- Artist pages with portfolio lightbox
- Service pages for tattoos and piercings
- A page per location
- Context-aware WhatsApp links
- Aftercare blog in MDX
