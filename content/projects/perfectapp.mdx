## Project Overview

PerfectApp is a Smart Data Center designed to centralize information from different actors in the commercial execution chain — field teams, distributors, retailers and shoppers — and present it as actionable intelligence.

## Key Features

### Centralized Dashboards
- Single-pane dashboards that aggregate KPIs across channels, SKUs and geographies.

### Insights & Segmentation
- Built-in modules to identify market segments, growth opportunities and risk signals.

### Data Science & MUTE Methodology
- Data models and a repeatable methodology (MUTE) to convert raw data into strategic actions.

### Reporting & Automation
- Scheduled exports, alerts and automated summaries for stakeholders.

## How It Helps

- **Decisions Informed:** Timely, clear and precise data to support strategic choices.
- **Identify Opportunities & Threats:** Detect trends and anomalies faster.
- **Optimize Resources:** Convert data into prioritized actions to reduce waste.

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- **Next.js** for fast SSR/SSG pages and interactive dashboards.
- **React** with modular components for charts and tables.

### Backend & Data
- **Prisma** and Node.js for data access and integrations.
- ETL pipelines to normalize data from multiple sources.

## Impact

- Centralized reporting reduces time-to-insight and improves field execution.
- Teams gain a shared source of truth for decisions and planning.
